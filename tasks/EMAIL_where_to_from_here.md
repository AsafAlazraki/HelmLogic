Subject: HelmLogic, the build is done. Now we reshape the roadmap.

Hi team,

Big milestone this week. The roadmap we set out to build on HelmLogic is essentially done. Every planned feature column, plus the backlog we pulled in on top, is built and tested on our dev environment. A year of planned work, complete.

**👉 Open the Roadmap and you'll see it. It's gone green.** That's the best way to take in how much has landed.

So here's the shift. The roadmap has been a feature-build list, and that phase is basically finished. It now needs to become something different: a map of the stages that take us from "built" to "live and growing." Here's how I see those stages, in order.

**First: get it into people's hands properly**

Before anything else, a short list of things that have to be right for go-live:

- **Email / SMTP.** Every email the system needs (quotes, variations, reminders) is ready to go, but sending is still switched off until we sort the Office 365 setup and sign off the sender domain. This is the most visible thing still dark, and the one most worth sorting this week.
- **The existing ServiceHub data migration.** The current ServiceHub holds live data that needs to come across. This is its own exercise, with its own owner, cutoff date, and parallel-run plan. It shouldn't hide inside "launch tasks."
- **The multi-user quote bug.** You can't currently edit a quote someone else created. I've found why and I'm fixing it this weekend. It matters for any dealership where more than one person touches a deal.
- **UAT.** The most valuable thing we can do now is actually use the system in anger, real quotes, real customers, real contracts, and find where it bites. End-to-end use finds what screen-by-screen checking doesn't.

**Then: the growth layer**

Once it's solid and live, the roadmap reshapes toward getting it adopted and used well:

- **Marketing.** Positioning and rollout of HelmLogic itself.
- **Enablement and training.** Getting the team confident and fast, training sessions per role, the materials that go with them.
- **Sub-dealer rollouts.** Bringing sub-dealers onto the platform. This is a meaningful part of the model and it hasn't been properly exercised yet, so it gets real attention in this phase.
- **The bigger integrations.** Shopify and the Revolution data work, which are gated on access we're still chasing rather than on build effort.

**A few calls still to make**

Some things are waiting on decisions rather than work: the e-signature approach, which states we operate in and the compliance text that follows, and the sender-domain and SMTP setup above. The faster we make those calls, the faster they clear.

**Where this leaves us**

The build is done. The next stages are production-readiness first, then growth: marketing, enablement, training, sub-dealer rollouts. The reshaped roadmap should reflect that, and I'd like us to shape those stages together rather than me guessing at them.

Proud of where this has landed. Let's get it live.

Cheers,
Asaf
