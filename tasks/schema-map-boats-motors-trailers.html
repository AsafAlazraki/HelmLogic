How a quote assembles: variant (hull inc-GST ladder + pdTier) + motorMenu slot → motor row (priceLevels) + slot rigging kit (org riggingKits) + slot prop (org serviceParts) + trailerMenu → trailer + ATM-band rego + dealer fit + fit-up. Motor-only quotes skip the hull and compose from the motor row's own columns (Install - Sell, Engine Removals, masterAccessories, mpfOptionBands). Finalized quotes snapshot everything into users/{uid}/quotes/{quoteId} — prices never drift after finalize.
