Two releases have gone live since my last update. The short version: HelmLogic now prices every boat package exactly the way the Master Price File does, to the dollar, and every single request the team submitted through the system has been closed: built, fixed, or proven already working.

Quotes now match the Display Sheet, to the dollar

Mark put the system through a real field test: the same SP560 package priced in the Display Sheet and in HelmLogic. The gap he found was traced to the cent, fixed at the root for every boat in the catalogue, and re-proven: the exact configuration rebuilt in HelmLogic and finalised to a customer PDF at $103,731, identical to his sheet on every line: hull, pre-delivery and installation, motor, rigging, prop, trailer, both regos and all eight options. Pre-delivery and installation now appears as its own line on the customer document, exactly as your sheet presents it. That test is exactly what we asked for, and the system is better for it. Keep them coming.

Every submitted request, closed

All 26 items the team logged through Feature Tracking were dealt with in this release. Highlights of what changed:

A new ADMINISTRATION step in the quote flow gathers the paperwork in one place: rego selections and rego numbers, the 6 year warranty and service plan, trade-in, delivery, insurance, finance, and a driver's licence upload.

Rebates now show on the right part of the quote: hull rebates on the boat step, motor rebates on the motor step.

The customer PDF reads cleaner: Factory Options under their own heading, and the document says Northside Marine where it used to say Dealer.

Catalogue quality of life: remove a single dealer-fit item instead of clearing all, upload photos to dealer-fit parts, add your own option headings for any brand, bigger option text, and rego sticker pricing in the system.

The Catalog Explorer's dealer-fit section now shows everything the Master Price File drives on quotes, priced with the same figures quotes charge, organised into collapsible sections, with the MPF's internal bookkeeping sections clearly marked as hidden from customers.

Usage & Activity

There is a new page in the menu for management: Usage & Activity. From this release forward the system keeps a complete record of how it is used: who logged on, how long they were genuinely active, every page, every click, and what each person has and has not touched, with reports you can filter by person and time and export to PDF. When we make decisions about what to build next, or when there is a question about whether something is being used, we will be working from the record rather than from impressions.
