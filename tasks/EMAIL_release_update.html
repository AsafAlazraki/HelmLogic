Hi all,

Following on from my last email: the release is live, and it went out carrying something much bigger than planned. The short version: the entire Master Price File now lives inside HelmLogic, verified value for value against the original, with the evidence attached.

What happened

Last week I was made aware of discussions about bringing in offshore data teams to migrate information between systems. Rather than let that conversation run, I pointed our development capability at it. In the days that followed, the entire Master Price File, all 17 linked workbooks, was ingested, decoded and migrated into HelmLogic: 36,551 database writes with zero errors, every single write logged with its before and after state. The migration NSM was going to pay an external team to do, and then manage, is done.

The proof

It was proven, not assumed. 34,512 automated checks confirmed HelmLogic holds the Master Price File value for value, and that same verification now re-runs automatically every night, so accuracy is a standing guarantee rather than a one-off claim. As the final test, the same boat package was priced by the Master Price File's own formulas and by HelmLogic side by side: a Highfield SP560 with motor, rigging, trailer, dealer fit and rego, seven line items, every cent identical, $79,022 against $79,022. The full evidence report is attached, and it is built to be checked: open it and verify any line you like.

Credit where it is due

Mr Colin Kean. His price file went through the most demanding audit it will ever face, and across tens of thousands of live figures the genuine defects could be counted on one hand. The reason this migration succeeded so quickly is that the foundation he built over years was sound. HelmLogic is not a replacement for that work. It is that work, given a stronger home.

What happens next

1. Brand captains. The machine has done its part; the data is in, configured and verified. The next layer of refinement belongs to the people who know how each brand should sell: which motor leads on each hull, which options sit front and centre, how the brand reads on a customer's quote. That baseline is now in their hands to tune. People decide, machines verify.

2. Motor quoting. Bill and I spoke over the weekend about where to point the next phase. With every motor, rigging kit, prop and price level now in the system, motor quoting is the fastest win on the table and the highest-volume quote the counter writes. And once this quicker win is locked down, the same motor quoting plugs straight into the boat side of quotes, so the work compounds.

3. Your most urgent requirement. Beyond that, the priority call belongs to the team. Talk it through among yourselves: what is the single most urgent thing NSM needs this system to do next? Agree on it as a group, report it back, and we will point the work at it.
